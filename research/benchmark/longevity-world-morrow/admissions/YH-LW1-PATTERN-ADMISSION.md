# YH-LW1｜Pattern Admission Decision

## Admission principle

Admit a pattern only when it strengthens Yuanli Health’s strategic objective:

> **Health Optionality through a Sovereign Principal Health Office that reduces management burden while preserving final authority.**

Do not admit patterns merely because they are premium, fashionable or technologically impressive.

## Decisions

| Pattern | Decision | Why | Yuanli translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthspan rather than elder-care framing | ADMIT | Reframes health around preserved future capability and choice | Health Optionality |
| Assessment → Plan → Execute → Reassess | STRONG ADMIT | Converts data into managed state transition | Evidence → Thesis → Battle → Action → Outcome |
| One relationship owner / Health Coach | STRONG ADMIT | Reduces user coordination burden | AI-native Health Steward |
| Medical + lifestyle integration | ADMIT | Combines authority with execution frequency | Clinician Gate + Managed Operations |
| 12-week structured transformation plan | STRONG ADMIT | Clear bounded promise, measurable baseline and reassessment | 30/90-Day Managed Battle |
| Membership recurring relationship | ADMIT | Maintains continuity after intervention | Managed Health Office recurring relationship |
| Community for retention | PARTIAL ADMIT | Can increase consistency, but not core to Principal value | Optional engagement layer |
| AI extends human touch | ADMIT | Improves frequency without pretending AI owns medical authority | AI Health Brain + Steward |
| Same methodology across centres | STRONG ADMIT | Productisation comes from operating model, not building copy | One Product × Many Principals × Zero Fork |
| Physical destination as category brand | PARTIAL ADMIT | Strong trust/experience, but asset-heavy | Partner network / selective flagship only |
| Own large real-estate footprint | DO NOT COPY | Capital intensity is not Yuanli’s comparative advantage | Partner, do not own |

| Weak-evidence longevity interventions | REJECT / EVIDENCE GATE | Conflicts with evidence and clinical authority constitution | Preserve strict evidence/authority gate |
| Wellness outcome = causal learning | REJECT | Multi-intervention correlation is not attribution | Require ACT → OUT → Attribution → LRN |

## Strategic admission summary

### Strongest patterns to absorb now

1. **Managed state transition rather than data dashboard.**
2. **One relationship owner rather than fragmented specialists.**
3. **Medical authority + lifestyle execution as separate but coordinated layers.**
4. **Bounded 12-week / 90-day battle with baseline and reassessment.**
5. **Replicate methodology and operating model, not custom projects.**

### Patterns to absorb later / selectively

- community
- physical destination experience
- partner marketplace
- employer / insurer distribution

### Patterns Yuanli Health should not copy

- asset-heavy centre ownership as the core moat
- modality-led merchandising
- unproven longevity claims
- treating more data as proof of causality

## Strategic differentiation

MORROW’s visible edge is **Reality Network density**.

Yuanli Health’s intended edge is **Principal continuity**:

`Sovereign Memory → Evidence → Decision → Action → Outcome → Attribution → Learning → Reuse`

Therefore the correct relationship is not direct imitation.

> **MORROW-like operators should become high-quality Reality Network nodes that a Yuanli Health Principal can use without losing sovereignty, context or longitudinal learning.**

## Final candidate verdict

`PARTIAL_STRONG_ADMIT`

Strongly admit the operating patterns around managed preventive care, coordination and repeatability; reject the assumption that physical infrastructure or premium longevity modalities themselves are the strategic moat.
